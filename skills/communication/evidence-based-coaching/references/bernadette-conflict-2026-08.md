# Case study: Bernadette conflict (2026-08-07)

Attila (PM) announced an async-first workflow to a university project group. Bernadette (groupmate) pushed back citing professor-mandated daily syncs. Attila's read: "she misunderstood my message." Analysis proved otherwise.

## Key evidence quotes (Tagalog, kept verbatim)

- Her restatement of his position: *"kung ang stand mo is pag may proposal kana gagawa or iaapply un, i think indi sha applicable"* → she UNDERSTOOD and disagreed. Verdict: disagreement, not misunderstanding.
- Her explicit acknowledgment: *"kaya nga pinapaintindi ko sayo… pero may iba kang stand."*
- Her actual axis (repeated 3×): *"sa daily meetings naman na sinasabi ni louie niraraise ko boi"* — cadence, not deliverables.
- Her bias (conceded to user): *"behind na tau"* assumed non-performance without asking; *"di pwede petiks petiks"* = character framing; never asked "what have you done?"
- His enabling omission: message said *"ako na bahala mag-decide kung kailangan ng meeting"* with ZERO mention of the professor's mandated daily syncs → her "di applicable" reading was rational.
- His excuse-shaped sentence: *"kasi may Software Engineering pa tayo"* placed directly after "avoid unnecessary meetings" — intention clean, wording functioned as justification. Negative-ROI sentence; her "same prof lang yan" rebuttal was correct on merits.

## Confidence estimates delivered

- P(she misunderstood) ≈ 0.05 (paraphrase in her own message)
- P(understood + disagreed) ≈ 0.90
- P(behind on cadence) ≈ 0.85; P(behind on deliverables) ≈ 0.20
- P(her intent = undermine) ≈ 0.10; P(flag risk) ≈ 0.85

## Patterns observed (user side)

- 6 emotional reactions mapped 1:1 to his documented vulnerabilities (competence, being-misunderstood, fairness, intellectual frustration, childhood disrespect sensitivity) — triggers fired on misreads in ≥3 of 6.
- Re-litigated the same exchange 5+ rounds: misunderstanding → bias → false accusation → social proof → "what's she fussing about" (refusal to update after being told the answer 3×).
- Defense pattern: per-sentence defense ("I didn't use it as an excuse") instead of whole-message effect.

## What resolved it (the fix, not the argument)

1. Accept her valid core: cadence requirement is real; group agreed to Sprint Daily Updates (Discord, 3-question format) in the Aug 5 minutes — the mechanism already exists.
2. Show receipts: pin schedule + interviews; start posting daily updates today.
3. Convert challenger → ally: accept her offer ("if u want, i can help u") with a concrete role (tracker owner).
4. Reply draft (plain English, 4 moves): keep position / accept her point / show work / give her a role.

## Generalizable lesson

In institutional contexts (school, graded process), other groups' behavior is a *compliance/risk probe* about the authority's ambiguous requirements — not evidence of efficacy. Dismissing it as "social proof" misses its informational function. The winning move is supplying a better metric (checklist), not arguing about her metric.
