# Two-Lives Script Template

## Structure (85 seconds)

```
[HOOK | 0:00–0:08]
Two people started with the exact same amount of money.
Same age. Same opportunities.
But one decision changed the rest of their lives.

[CHILDHOOD | 0:08–0:18]
Both dreamed of success.
But as teenagers, one spent on toys and gadgets.
The other saved and learned.

[TEENAGE | 0:18–0:30]
Their paths diverged.
One: partying, paycheck to paycheck.
Other: books, courses, skills, investments.
Failures started. Friends laughed.

[YOUNG ADULT | 0:30–0:45]
The risk-taker kept going.
Side hustle #1: failed.
Trading: lost money.
Course: learned something.
Startup: failed.
But every failure taught him.
Every skill stayed forever.

[COMFORT ZONE | 0:45–0:53]
The other looked happy.
Nice life. Nice things.
But behind the scenes: debt, no savings.
One emergency away from disaster.

[MIDDLE AGE | 0:53–1:05]
Years passed.
Risk-taker: multiple income streams, assets working for him.
Saver: still depending on one paycheck.
The gap kept growing.

[OLD AGE | 1:05–1:15]
One still worried about bills.
The other traveled, gave to others, enjoyed freedom.
Not because he was lucky.
Because he was prepared.

[CTA | 1:15–1:25]
The biggest risk isn't failing.
It's avoiding every opportunity to grow.
Your investments may fail.
But the skills you gain pay you for life.
Smart risks today, or desperate ones tomorrow.
```

## Timing Formula
- Hook: 8s (setup curiosity)
- Each life stage: 10-15s
- Risk-taker's journey gets the most time (15s) — it's the emotional core
- CTA: 10s (land the message)
- Total: 85s

## Word Budget
- Target: 2.0-2.5 words/second = 170-210 words for 85s
- At 150 WPM speaking rate with pauses
- edge-tts at -15% rate produces ~128 WPM → ~166 words in 85s
- Use atempo to compress TTS to exact video duration
