# Clinic CBA research grounding (verified 2026-08)

Sources for benefit-side estimates when the CBA target is a clinic/medical practice.

## No-show rates
- Literature range 4.5%–42% by setting. PH tertiary hospital (Suk 2021, PMC8005203): examinations 30.1%, treatment/surgery 16.6%, consultations 4.5%.
- Rural US health centers average ~42% missed (Shour 2023, PMC11149957).
- Practical conservative default for a small PH clinic: 15–20%.

## Reminder effectiveness
- SMS/text reminders cut no-shows 20–30% (Curogram industry summary).
- Up to 38% reduction reported (DoctorConnect).
- ~33% of missed appointments = patient simply forgot (Dialog Health 2022 survey).
- Self-scheduling tools reduce no-shows ~29% (Dialog Health).
- Manual reminder calls "eat hours each week" of front-desk time (Zocdoc front desk guide).

## Cost anchors (PH, 2026)
- Front-desk wage ≈ ₱120/hr.
- Typical visit revenue ₱400–1,500 (use ₱600 conservative).

## Default assumption set (Attila's clinic CMS CBA — reuse/sanity-check future CBAs)
30 visits/day × 22 days; 20% no-show; 25% recovered via reminders; ₱600/visit;
2 hrs/day staff time saved at ₱120/hr; +2 new patients/mo via online booking.
Result: ~₱315k/yr tangible benefits vs ₱115k total 3-yr costs
(dev ₱54k = 180h × ₱300 + ₱19k one-time + ₱14.1k/yr ongoing) → net ₱830k, payback ~4.4 mo.
Worst case (costs +10%, benefits 80%) still nets ~₱630k.
