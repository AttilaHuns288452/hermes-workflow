# Romance Character Expansion Guide

## Why Romance Characters Matter
Battle shonen characters attract a core anime audience, but romance/shoujo characters broaden reach to users who don't watch action anime. Adding 40-50 romance characters from popular series significantly increases the quiz's appeal.

## Top Romance Series & Characters

### Must-Have Series (highest recognition)
| Series | Key Characters | Why They Work |
|--------|---------------|---------------|
| **Toradora!** | Taiga Aisaka, Ryuuji Takasu | Quintessential tsundere waifu, relatable male lead |
| **Your Lie in April** | Kaori Miyazono, Kousei Arima | Emotional depth, music theme, tragic romance |
| **Clannad** | Nagisa Furukawa, Tomoya Okazaki | Iconic, emotionally devastating pairing |
| **Fruits Basket** | Tohru Honda, Kyo Sohma, Yuki Sohma | Large cast, zodiac theme, multiple appealing characters |
| **Horimiya** | Kyouko Hori, Izumi Miyamura | Realistic romance, hidden personalities |
| **Kimi ni Todoke** | Sawako Kuronuma, Shouta Kazehaya | Slow-burn, pure-hearted appeal |
| **Maid-Sama!** | Misaki Ayuzawa, Takumi Usui | Strong FMC, charismatic MMC |
| **Ao Haru Ride** | Futaba Yoshioka, Kou Mabuchi | Nostalgic high school romance |
| **Lovely Complex** | Risa Koizumi, Atsushi Otani | Comedy + height difference couple |
| **Nisekoi** | Chitoge Kirisaki, Kosaki Onodera, Raku Ichijo | Love triangle, comedy harem |
| **Bloom Into You** | Yuu Koito, Touko Nanami | Yuri romance, well-written |
| **My Happy Marriage** | Miyo Saimori, Kiyoka Kudo | Cinderella story, supernatural elements |
| **A Sign of Affection** | Yuki Itose, Itsuomi Nagi | Deaf protagonist, wholesome |
| **Dangers in My Heart** | Anna Yamada, Kyotaro Ichikawa | Edgy comedy, growing popularity |
| **Skip & Loafer** | Mitsumi Iwakura, Sousuke Shima | Country girl in city, wholesome |
| **Insomniacs After School** | Ganta Nakami, Isaki Magari | Shared secret, photography, chill vibes |
| **More Than Married Couple** | Akari Watanabe, Jirou Yakuin | Fake marriage trope |

## AniList Image Search Tips
Romance character names are usually searchable on AniList using standard English names. Try both Western and Japanese name order:

- "Miyazono Kaori" (also try)
